HOW THE MAGIC WORKS

Compare the naive keyword-frequency approach of 1997 with the revolutionary Random Surfer Markov chain model introduced by Google in 1998.

📝

Keyword Frequency (1997)

Syntactic Text Search
  • 1 Search word extraction: Extracts the first entered term and trims extra whitespace.
  • 2 Whole-word matching: Uses word boundary checks (\bword\b) so "pizza" won't match "pizzas" or "pizzeria".
  • 3 Case-insensitive counting: Ignores capitalisation differences ("PIZZA" = "pizza").
  • 4 Descending sorting: The page with the most repeated words is ranked #1. (Extremely easy to spam!).
🌊

Random Surfer / PageRank (1998)

Topological Link Equity
  • 1 Random initialization: The virtual surfer lands on a randomly picked page.
  • 2 85% Link Following: Follows an outgoing hyperlink at random (damping factor $d = 0.85$).
  • 3 15% Random Jump: The surfer gets bored and jumps to any random page on the web ($1 - d = 0.15$).
  • 4 100,000 Moves: Visit counts converge into the stationary probability distribution of importance.
